What VolleyRef does

  • Build a team roster with names, jersey numbers, and availability
  • Generate a complete work list for up to 3 sets — down ref, table (book, libero tracker, score flipper), and two line judges
  • Spreads work fairly; no player repeats the same job two sets in a row (line judge excepted)
  • Saves every list to History, and lets you share or print it
  • Works completely offline — your data stays on your device

Privacy

VolleyRef collects no data. Everything stays on your device. Read the full Privacy Policy.
